👋Who we are

Great Panda Escape is published by Kyrac Studio. This policy explains what happens to information when you play the game on Android. Questions: kyracstudiosupport@gmail.com

📱What stays on your phone

The game saves these in its own storage on your phone:

  • Your scores, best run, Jungle Rank and missions
  • Coins, Jade Gems, surfboards, upgrades and Wardrobe items
  • Your settings (sound, music, vibration, graphics, tilt steering)
  • Your Daily Gift progress
  • Your answer to the privacy question (players in Europe)
  • Which purchases have already been delivered, so nothing is given or charged twice

We do not receive a copy of this. If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the game's saved data in your phone's backup. That backup is made and kept by Google, not by us.

To decide whether to show the privacy question, the game checks on your phone which country your SIM card or mobile network belongs to, and your time zone. This check stays on your phone; it is not sent to us.

📊Game stats (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase, a Google service, to understand how the game is played. It receives:

  • Game events, such as a run starting and ending, distance, score, what ended the run, revives, opening the Shop or the Wardrobe, trying or buying an outfit, watching an optional ad, claiming the Daily Gift, and tapping a reminder
  • Purchase events (which item and its price), recorded when a purchase completes
  • A random ID that Firebase gives to this install of the game (the "app instance ID"), and basic device details such as the phone model, Android version, language and app version
  • An approximate location (country or city) that Google works out from your connection

The game does not send the advertising ID to Google Analytics, and it never sends your name, email or contacts. We use these stats only in summaries, to fix bugs, keep runs fair and decide what to improve. Google keeps this data for no longer than the retention period set in our Analytics account (at most 14 months).

We also use Firebase Remote Config, which lets us adjust a few game settings (such as how often ads appear between runs) without a new app update. It uses a Firebase installation ID and basic details such as your country, language, time zone and Android version. It runs only when game stats are allowed.

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

🎬Ads (Unity Ads)

The game is free and is paid for by ads from Unity Ads. Full-screen ads may appear between runs, never during a run. Ads that give a reward (for example a second chance, or trying an outfit for one run) are always your choice. There are no banner ads.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Unity collects data from your phone, such as the advertising ID, device and network details (including the IP address and an approximate location), and how you interact with the ads. Unity may use this data to show ads that match your interests ("personalised ads").

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (on most phones: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

🛒Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases (No Ads, Starter Pack and gem packs) are made through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details. The game receives the details it needs to deliver what you bought and to restore No Ads and the Starter Pack on a new phone with the same Google account.

🔔Reminders (notifications)

If you allow notifications, the game may remind you about your Daily Gift, missions, your best score, Today's Pick, or simply that the panda misses you. At most one a day, never at night, and they stop after a week without playing.

These reminders are planned and shown by your phone itself. No server is involved and nothing about them is sent to us (only a tap on a reminder is counted in the game stats).

Turn them off at any time: Settings → Apps → Great Panda Escape → Notifications. You can also turn off just one kind (for example "Daily Gift").

🌍Players in Europe (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

When you first open the game, we ask whether we may use game stats and personalised ads. Nothing is sent to Google Analytics before you answer, and ads start only after your answer.

  • Allow all: game stats on, personalised ads on.
  • Only necessary: game stats off, and ads are not personalised. You still see ads, just less matched to you.
  • Choose: turn each one on or off yourself.

Change your answer at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy choices.

The legal basis for game stats and personalised ads is your consent. Basic ad delivery, fraud prevention and delivering your purchases rely on our legitimate interest in running and funding the game and on performing our agreement with you.

🤝Who receives data

We do not sell your personal data. Data goes only to the services named above: Google (Firebase and Google Play) and Unity. Their servers may be outside your country, and they protect data in transit with encryption (HTTPS).

🧒Children

Great Panda Escape is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. The game is listed for players aged 13 and over. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will help.

✅Your choices and rights

  • Players in Europe: change your answer in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Ads: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Reminders: turn them off in your phone's notification settings.
  • Everything saved on your phone: uninstall the game, or use Settings → Apps → Great Panda Escape → Storage → Clear data. This deletes your progress.

We do not hold your name, email or an account, so we cannot link game stats or ad data to you personally. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use. Write to us and we will help as far as we can. For data held by Google, you can also use myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy; for data held by Unity, use the choices in Unity's privacy policy (linked above). In Europe, you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

📝Changes to this policy

If the game starts handling information differently, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches players.
